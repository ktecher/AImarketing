# Compliance guardrails

Hard rules. These are not tone preferences and the advisor treats violations as **blockers** — content that cannot be published — rather than as advice.

## Never generate political content

No political topics, figures, parties, events, or positions. Not as a subject, not as a joke, not as a hook, not as an analogy. This includes regional politics and anything that could be read as taking a side.

## Never use religion as a marketing angle

Religious occasions may be referenced only as **neutral calendar facts** — "Eid hours", "Ramadan offers" — in the same register a shop sign would use.

Never:

- Religious language, imagery, or quotation used to sell something
- Piety framed as a reason to buy
- A product tied to religious observance or virtue
- Timing a promotion to a religious moment in a way that trades on its significance

Ramadan and Eid are commercially enormous in this market and completely normal to run offers around. The line is between *acknowledging the season* and *using the faith*.

Two specific traps worth naming, because they are easy to walk into by accident:

- Food and drink imagery during Ramadan daylight hours.
- A loud, celebratory promotion during a period of national or public mourning.

## Advertising claims

- **No unsubstantiable superlatives** — "best price in Kuwait", "the fastest anywhere", "number one". Treat as a blocker.
- **No comparative claims against a named competitor.**
- **No guarantees** the company has not actually committed to — delivery times, stock availability, performance outcomes.
- **Every price, spec, and availability claim must trace to a catalog row.** If it is not in the data, it does not go in the copy.

## Scope

These rules apply to everything the system produces or reviews: generated copy, chatbot replies, video scripts, and drafts pasted in by a human for review. They apply identically in Arabic and English.

When something is close to the line, it is a blocker. A published mistake in this category is not recoverable by an edit.
