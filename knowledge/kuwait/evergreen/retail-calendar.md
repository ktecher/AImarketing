# Kuwait retail calendar

Stable year over year. This is the layer worth relying on — most of what "knows Kuwait" means in practice is here, not in this week's trends.

Dates that move with the Hijri calendar shift roughly 10–11 days earlier each Gregorian year. Always confirm the actual dates for the year you are planning; do not compute them.

## Fixed dates

| Occasion | Date | Retail character |
|---|---|---|
| National Day | 25 February | Patriotic themes, heavy decoration, national colours |
| Liberation Day | 26 February | Runs continuously with National Day as one two-day period |
| Hala Febrayer | Through February | Kuwait's own festival season — shopping promotions, events, city-wide campaigns. The biggest fixed retail window in the calendar |

## Moving dates

| Occasion | Timing | Retail character |
|---|---|---|
| Ramadan | Shifts ~11 days earlier annually. **In 2027, roughly February–March** | Daily rhythm inverts. Heavy evening and late-night activity |
| Eid al-Fitr | End of Ramadan | Gifting, new purchases, family spending |
| Eid al-Adha | ~10 weeks after Eid al-Fitr | Gifting, travel |

**Note the 2027 collision:** Ramadan will overlap National Day, Liberation Day, and Hala Febrayer. Plan for it rather than discovering it.

## Seasonal windows

- **Back to School** — late summer into September.
- **Summer travel** — a long outbound period; much of the audience is abroad. Electronics and travel goods move earlier.
- **Black Friday** — observed, though framing varies locally; some retailers use "White Friday."

## Ramadan behaviour

Usage shifts later, and this is well documented directionally.

- Smartphone usage in Kuwait peaks **9 PM – 1 AM** during Ramadan, later than any other month (Ipsos MENA, 2023 — the most credible timing figure available, and now several years old).
- Three commonly cited daily windows: pre-Iftar late afternoon, post-Iftar evening, and Suhoor in the small hours.
- Video consumption reportedly rises substantially. **Primary source unverified** — treat as directional only.

Treat Ramadan as a switchable mode in scheduling rather than something to handle by hand each year.

## Weekend

The Kuwaiti weekend is **Friday and Saturday**. Thursday evening behaves like a Friday evening elsewhere.
